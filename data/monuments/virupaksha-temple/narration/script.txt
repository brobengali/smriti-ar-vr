Virupaksha Temple stands on the sacred southern bank of the Tungabhadra River in Hampi, Karnataka. Originating in the seventh century as a modest Chalukyan sanctuary, it was expanded by the Vijayanagara emperors, most notably Krishnadevaraya in 1510, who erected its fifty-metre eastern Rajagopuram and pillared Ranga Mandapa. While the city of Vijayanagara fell in 1565, worship at Virupaksha has continued unbroken for over one thousand years.
