The Stone Chariot of Vittala Temple is a sixteenth-century shrine carved to resemble the ceremonial procession ratha of the mythical bird-mount Garuda. Built by Emperor Krishnadevaraya following his victory in the Kalinga campaign, the chariot originally possessed a brick-and-mortar shikhara tower, which remained intact into the mid-nineteenth century as recorded in Alexander Greenlaw's early photographs, before being dismantled by colonial conservators around 1890 to protect the delicate granite wheels beneath.
