The Lotus Mahal, nestled within Hampi's Zenana Enclosure, is an exemplar of secular Vijayanagara palace architecture. Combining the lobed, cusped arches of Deccani Islamic design with stepped multi-roof towers typical of Hindu temples, this two-storey pavilion functioned as a summer retreat and council chamber. Concealed terracotta conduits within its twenty-four stone pillars carried circulating water to cool the building during the blazing Deccan heat.
